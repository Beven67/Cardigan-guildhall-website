Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Cardigan Guildhall Market

General Information

About the Market

Cardigan Guildhall Market is usually open Monday to Saturday from 9:00am to 5:00pm. The market is normally closed on Sundays, but opening times may change during holidays or special events.

The market is located inside the Guildhall in the centre of Cardigan, Ceredigion. It can be accessed from the High Street, College Row, and the Greenfield Square car park side.

Yes, parking is available nearby. Greenfield Square car park is located close to the rear of the market, with other public car parks available around Cardigan town centre.

Yes, the market is set over two floors and includes both stairs and lift access. This helps make the building easier to visit for wheelchair users, older visitors, and anyone who needs step-free access.

For Traders

Becoming a Vendor

If you are interested in becoming a vendor, you can apply through the Contact Us page. The market welcomes independent traders, local businesses, food stalls, artists, makers, and sellers with unique products.

Stall rental costs may vary depending on stall size, location, and how long the vendor wants to trade for. For accurate pricing and availability, traders should contact the market directly through the Contact Us page.

Vendors can sell a wide range of products including handmade crafts, jewellery, fresh food, local produce, artwork, gifts, clothing, home décor, and specialist items. The market is best suited to products that are high quality, creative, and suitable for a family-friendly environment.

Temporary and pop-up stall opportunities may be available during busy periods, seasonal events, or special market days. Traders should contact the market team to ask about short-term spaces and current availability.

Events & Activities

Events at the Market

Visitors can find out about upcoming events through the Events page, the market’s social media pages, and local announcements. Seasonal markets, food events, workshops, and community activities are usually shared online.

Event hosting may be possible depending on the type of event, space needed, and market availability. Anyone interested in hosting an event should use the Contact Us page to discuss ideas, dates, and requirements.

Yes, the market may host seasonal events such as Christmas markets, summer fairs, food days, craft events, and community celebrations. These events help bring more variety to the market and give visitors something new to enjoy.

For Visitors

Visiting the Market

Many vendors accept card, contactless, and cash payments, but payment options can vary between stalls. It is useful to bring both cash and card so you are prepared when visiting different vendors.

Well-behaved dogs are generally welcome, but they should be kept on a lead and under control at all times. Some food areas or individual stalls may have their own rules, so visitors should be respectful of vendor requests.

Yes, the market includes food vendors offering meals, snacks, drinks, and fresh local options. Visitors can find a mix of comforting food, street food, sweet treats, and lighter choices depending on which stalls are open.

Yes, public toilet facilities are available inside the market building. The Guildhall Market is listed as having public toilets on each floor, making visits easier for families, older visitors, and people spending longer at the market.
