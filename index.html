About Our Market

A Historic Market in the Heart of Cardigan

Right in the middle of cardigan, the guildhall market is one of those places that feels genuenly local from the moment you walk in.

Set inside a historic building, it is home to a mix of small and indipendent stalls that sells everything from handmade items to fresh food and everyday bits you dont know you need.
