About This Vendor

We are a local food stall based inside the Guildhall Market in Cardigan, serving simple, comforting food made properly. We specialise in freshly prepared jacket potatoes with a range of classic and filling toppings for both locals and visitors looking for something warm and satisfying.

We focus on using local produce, especially locally sourced potatoes, which gives our food a more authentic homemade feel rather than a typical fast-food approach. Everything is prepared fresh, with a strong focus on quality, consistency, and generous portions.

Although we are still relatively new to the market, we have quickly become part of the local community. We believe good food does not need to be complicated just fresh ingredients, friendly service, and meals that people genuinely enjoy.
