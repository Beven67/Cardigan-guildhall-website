Under the Laurel

[Vendor Category - Jewellery]

About This Vendor

Under the Laurel is an independent handmade business based inside the Guildhall Market in Cardigan. They specialise in handmade jewellery, artwork, gifts, pet products, and nature-inspired creations designed with a strong focus on individuality and creativity. What makes the business stand out is its artistic style, with many products featuring hand-drawn designs inspired by animals, flowers, insects, and the natural world.

Their range includes handmade earrings, necklaces, artwork, greeting cards, decorative pieces, and pet-related products, all carefully designed and created with attention to detail. Many of their items are hand drawn and produced in small quantities, giving customers access to unique products rather than mass-produced designs. The business focuses heavily on quality craftsmanship, creativity, and handmade production, with products available both online and in their Guildhall Market shop.

Under the Laurel has become part of the growing creative community inside the Guildhall Market, regularly releasing new collections and seasonal handmade products. Their stall is located on the lower floor of the market and has built a strong identity around colourful artwork, nature themes, and personalised handmade creations. Alongside jewellery and gifts, they also offer pet products and custom pet portraits, helping the business stand out from more traditional gift stalls inside the market. Under the Laurel
