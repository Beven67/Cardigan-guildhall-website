The Craft Wife of Teifi - Gwraig Grefft Teifi

[Vendor Category - Arts and crafts]

About This Vendor

The Craft Wife of Teifi is an independent handmade business based inside the Guildhall Market in Cardigan. The stall specialises in handmade crafts, gifts, clothing, home décor, herbal products, and locally inspired items designed to offer customers something unique and personal. What makes the business stand out is its strong focus on handmade quality, creativity, and supporting local makers and independent products.

Their range includes handmade gifts, decorative pieces, herbal remedies, clothing, seasonal items, and reversible Kantha quilt products. The business focuses on carefully selected products with an emphasis on craftsmanship, originality, and quality rather than mass-produced items. Many of the products are handmade or sourced from small independent suppliers, helping customers discover unique gifts and locally inspired creations inside the market.

The Craft Wife of Teifi has become a recognised part of the Guildhall Market community and has expanded into a larger unit inside the market due to its growing popularity. The stall is known for regularly updating its stock with new handmade products, seasonal gifts, and exclusive items that cannot easily be found elsewhere in Cardigan. Their welcoming atmosphere and focus on creativity have helped build a strong local customer base within the market.
