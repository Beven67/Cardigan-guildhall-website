Street Food Morocco

[Vendor Category - Food stall]

About This Vendor

Street Food Morocco brings authentic Moroccan street food to the heart of the Guildhall Market in Cardigan. Specialising in traditional Moroccan flavours, the stall offers freshly prepared meals inspired by the food markets and street vendors of Marrakech. What makes them stand out is their combination of rich spices, homemade cooking, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere that gives customers a different food experience compared to typical takeaway food.

Their menu includes a variety of Moroccan wraps, rice boxes, couscous dishes, grilled meats, vegan options, and traditional Moroccan drinks. The stall focuses on fresh preparation, bold flavours, and authentic ingredients that reflect traditional Moroccan cooking styles. Many of their dishes are homemade and prepared using carefully selected spices and fresh ingredients to maintain quality and consistency throughout the menu.

Street Food Morocco has become a popular part of the local market atmosphere, regularly serving customers during weekend market events and community gatherings. Their stall is based in the courtyard area of the market and has built a reputation for friendly service, colourful presentation, and food that offers something different within the Guildhall Market. Alongside traditional meat dishes, they also provide vegan-friendly meals, helping them appeal to a wider range of visitors.
