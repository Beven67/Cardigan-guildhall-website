About This Vendor

Rugs Wales is an independent business based inside the Guildhall Market, specialising in handmade rugs, traditional textiles, and unique home décor pieces inspired by Moroccan and Middle Eastern craftsmanship. The stall stands out for its colourful collections, detailed patterns, and authentic handmade products that bring character and warmth into any home.

Their range includes handmade rugs, cushions, throws, decorative textiles, and woven home accessories sourced from skilled artisans and traditional markets. Rugs Wales focuses on quality craftsmanship, durable materials, and carefully selected handmade products rather than factory-made home décor. Many of their items feature traditional weaving techniques and detailed handmade designs, making every piece feel unique and authentic.

Rugs Wales has become part of the growing variety of independent traders inside the Guildhall Market, offering customers something different from typical high-street homeware stores. Their stall is known for its colourful displays, traditional patterns, and welcoming atmosphere, helping visitors discover handmade home décor and textiles with a more personal and artistic feel.
