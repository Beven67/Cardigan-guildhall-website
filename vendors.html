Become a Vendor

Looking to become part of the Guildhall Market community? We are always looking for passionate local vendors, independent businesses, and creative traders to join the market. Whether you sell handmade products, fresh food, local produce, gifts, or unique creations, the market offers a welcoming space to grow your business and connect with both locals and visitors in Cardigan. By joining the market, vendors become part of a supportive community that values quality, creativity, and local businesses. If you are interested in becoming a vendor, please visit our Contact Us page to apply or learn more about available opportunities.
